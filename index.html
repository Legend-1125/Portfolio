Character Bio

I'm a Founding AI Automation Engineer at Trekka LLC, where I design multi-agent retrieval systems and voice-driven workflows that automate work banks and healthcare teams used to do by hand: identity verification, insurance eligibility, internal knowledge lookup. Most of it runs on AWS Bedrock, orchestrated with n8n, and grounded in vector search.

Before that I was building distributed cloud infrastructure: a serverless log-ingestion pipeline processing 5GB+ daily at 99.9% uptime, and CloudFormation automation that cut provisioning time by a third. That systems background is why the AI work holds up in production instead of staying a notebook demo.

Outside client work I grind side quests that force me to learn a layer I don't touch day-to-day, like dual-encryption cloud storage, real-time computer vision on a Raspberry Pi, or a movie-platform comparison tool with its own auth system. When I'm not shipping, I'm probably playing something that taught me half of this obsession with systems and feedback loops.
